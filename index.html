1. Choose your priority

2. Say what you need to do

Now make your priority more specific. What do you want to be able to do in English?

Examples:

  • Understand the main ideas in short university lectures.
  • Write clearer work emails.
  • Speak for two minutes about a familiar topic.
  • Read short articles without translating every sentence.

3. Set a short-term goal

Think about a small goal you can work on soon.

4. Check your real situation

A good goal needs to fit your life. Think about the time, resources, and support you have.

Time

How much time can you really spend learning English in a normal week?

Resources and support

What can you use? For example: your phone, internet, books, class materials, a friend, a colleague, a study group, or a digital tool.

5. Reality check

Look again at your goal. Ask yourself:

  • Can I do this with the time I have?
  • Do I have the resources or support I need?
  • Is the goal small enough to work on now?
Keep this: your priority, short-term goal, and real study situation. You will use them again in your final self-study plan.
