Record the feedback and decide

  
Write or paste each suggestion. Then choose what to do with it.

  

    
Use: I understand it, and it makes my writing better.

    Reject: I think it's wrong, or it doesn't fit my meaning.

    Investigate: I'm not sure. I need to check another source.

    
If you can't explain why a suggestion is right, don't use it yet.
